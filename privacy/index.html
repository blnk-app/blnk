Privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026

In short: BLNK collects no data about you or what you capture. There are no accounts, analytics, telemetry or ads. Screenshots stay on your computer until you decide to send them.

What is stored on your computer

  • Screenshots you save go to the folder you chose in the settings.
  • Screenshot history (thumbnails, originals for re-editing and links to uploaded files) is kept in the BLNK data folder.
  • Settings and the activity log are kept there too. Logs help track down errors; old ones are removed automatically.
  • Keys and passwords for your storage are kept in the system keychain (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain), not in BLNK's files.

None of this is sent anywhere. To remove the data, uninstall BLNK and delete its data folder.

When BLNK goes online

  • Update checks. BLNK asks GitHub whether a new version is out and downloads it from there. Nothing beyond the ordinary request is sent. Automatic checks can be turned off under “About”.
  • Uploading a screenshot. Only on your command and only to storage you set up yourself (S3, Cloudflare R2, your own HTTP address, FTP). We never see your screenshots, links or keys.
  • Deleting from the server from the history is also only on your command and only in your storage.

BLNK makes no other network connections.

Third-party services

The app and this website are hosted on GitHub. When you download, check for updates or visit the site, GitHub sees your IP address as with any request to it; what it does with it is described in its privacy statement. The site sets no cookies and uses no counters; the page only asks GitHub for the list of versions for the download buttons.

You choose the upload storage, and data there is governed by that service's terms.

Permissions

On macOS, BLNK asks for the “Screen Recording” permission; screenshots can't be taken without it. A screenshot is only taken on your hotkey or a menu command.

Changes

If features that need your data appear (accounts, for example), we will update this page in advance and change the date.

Contact

Questions go to Issues on GitHub.
