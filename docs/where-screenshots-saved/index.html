Where do screenshots go on Windows?

Screenshots taken with Win+PrtSc are saved in Pictures > Screenshots. PrtSc, Alt+PrtSc and Win+Shift+S don’t create a file: the screenshot stays on the clipboard until you paste it.

How to open the Screenshots folder

  1. Open File Explorer with Win+E.
  2. Select Pictures on the left.
  3. Open the Screenshots folder. The newest screenshots are at the end when you sort by date.

A screenshot on the clipboard: Win+V history

A screenshot on the clipboard lasts until you copy something else. To get an earlier one back, turn on clipboard history: press Win+V and select Turn on. After that, Win+V shows your recent screenshots and text, and you can paste any of them again. Clipboard settings are in Settings > System > Clipboard.

Snipping Tool screenshots

In Windows 11, Snipping Tool saves every snip to the Screenshots folder by itself. To turn off auto-save or pick another folder, open Snipping Tool and select See more > Settings.

How to change the screenshots folder

  1. Right-click the Screenshots folder and choose Properties.
  2. Open the Location tab.
  3. Select Move, pick a new folder and confirm. Win+PrtSc screenshots will be saved there from then on.

Restore Default on the same tab puts the folder back.

Where BLNK saves screenshots

BLNK saves a screenshot when you press Save or Ctrl+S — by default to the same Pictures > Screenshots folder. You can pick another folder in Settings → Saving. All your screenshots and recordings are also in BLNK’s history, which opens from the tray icon menu. See getting started.

Questions and answers

Why isn’t my screenshot saved?

You most likely pressed PrtSc, which only copies the screenshot to the clipboard. To get a file straight away, press Win+PrtSc. On a laptop, PrtSc sometimes works only together with Fn.

How do I move the Screenshots folder back?

Open the Screenshots folder’s properties, go to the Location tab and choose Restore Default.
