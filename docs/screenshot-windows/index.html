How to take a screenshot on Windows

Press PrtSc to copy the whole screen to the clipboard, then paste it into a chat or document. Win+PrtSc saves the screenshot as a file right away, and Win+Shift+S lets you select just the part of the screen you need.

Win is the Windows logo key. On the keyboard, PrtSc may read PrtScn, Prt Sc or Print Screen.

Whole screen: PrtSc and Win+PrtSc

  1. Press PrtSc. A screenshot of the whole screen goes to the clipboard.
  2. Paste it with Ctrl+V wherever you need it: a messenger, an email, a document or Paint.

If you need a file, press Win+PrtSc. The screen dims for a moment, and the screenshot is saved to Pictures > Screenshots.

One window: Alt+PrtSc

Click the window you want to capture and press Alt+PrtSc. Only that window goes to the clipboard, without the taskbar or the rest of the screen. Paste it with Ctrl+V.

Screenshot part of the screen: Win+Shift+S and Snipping Tool

  1. Press Win+Shift+S. The screen dims and a toolbar appears at the top.
  2. Pick a mode: rectangle, freeform, window or full screen.
  3. Select the area. The screenshot goes to the clipboard, and a notification appears in the corner: click it to open the screenshot in Snipping Tool, mark it up and save it.

You can also open Snipping Tool from the Start menu. In Windows 11 it saves every snip to the Screenshots folder by itself.

On a laptop

  • Where the key is. PrtSc is usually in the top row next to F12, or at the bottom right near the arrow keys. On compact laptops it often shares a key with another function.
  • The Fn key. If the PrtSc label is in a different colour, press it together with Fn: Fn+PrtSc. If the top-row keys work the other way round, check Fn Lock, which is usually Fn+Esc.
  • No PrtSc key at all. Press Win+Shift+S, or open the On-Screen Keyboard from the Start menu: it has a PrtScn key. On Surface tablets, press the power button together with volume up.

The other shortcuts — Win+PrtSc, Alt+PrtSc and Win+Shift+S — work on a laptop just as on a desktop PC, except that PrtSc sometimes needs Fn.

Windows 11, 10 and 7: the differences

  • Windows 11. PrtSc opens Snipping Tool by default so you can pick an area. To copy the whole screen instead, go to Settings > Accessibility > Keyboard.
  • Windows 10. PrtSc copies the whole screen. To make it open Snipping Tool, turn on the Print Screen shortcut in Settings > Ease of Access > Keyboard.
  • Windows 7. PrtSc and Alt+PrtSc work. Win+PrtSc and Win+Shift+S don’t exist there, and Snipping Tool opens from the Start menu.

Where screenshots are saved

Only Win+PrtSc creates a file, in Pictures > Screenshots, and so does Snipping Tool in Windows 11. The other methods put the screenshot on the clipboard. For details, including clipboard history and changing the folder, see where screenshots go on Windows.

If a screenshot doesn’t work

  • On a laptop, press PrtSc together with Fn, or switch Fn Lock.
  • Another program may be catching PrtSc, such as cloud storage or another screenshot tool. Close it or check its settings.
  • In Windows 11, check whether PrtSc opens Snipping Tool: the screenshot may be waiting for you to select an area.

Faster: a screenshot with markup and a link

If you take screenshots often, a dedicated app is handier. In BLNK you press PrtSc, select an area and add arrows, text or hide private details right over the selection. The screenshot is copied, saved or uploaded to your own storage with the link on the clipboard. See annotation and download.

Questions and answers

How do I take a screenshot without a PrtSc key?

Press Win+Shift+S and select an area. You can also open Snipping Tool from the Start menu, or the On-Screen Keyboard, which has a PrtScn key.

How do I save a screenshot as a file straight away?

Press Win+PrtSc. A full-screen screenshot is saved to Pictures > Screenshots. In Windows 11, Snipping Tool saves its snips there too.

Why does PrtSc open Snipping Tool?

That is the default in Windows 11. To make PrtSc copy the whole screen again, open Settings > Accessibility > Keyboard and turn off Use the Print Screen button to open screen snipping.
