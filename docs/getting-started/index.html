Getting started with BLNK

From installing to your first screenshot, a recording with sound and a link, in a few minutes.

Install

  1. Download the installer from the download page and run it.
  2. If Windows shows a SmartScreen warning, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. On the first screen, pick your hotkeys. A key that works gets a Works mark.

After installing, BLNK lives in the system tray by the clock. Its icon menu has screenshots, screen recording, history and settings.

Your first screenshot

For a screenshot without any app, Windows has built-in methods, collected in how to take a screenshot on Windows. With BLNK it’s faster:

  1. Press PrtSc and select an area. Shift+PrtSc captures the whole screen, Ctrl+PrtSc a window.
  2. Add arrows, text or hide private details right over the selection.
  3. Press Enter or double-click inside the frame to copy the screenshot. Use the panel buttons to save, upload or open it in the editor.

Ctrl+Shift+PrtSc repeats the last area. In the selection window, Shift+C snaps the frame to the content and M turns on a pixel ruler. All keys are in the Hotkeys guide.

Record your screen with sound

  1. Open Settings → Hotkeys and assign a key to Record screen. It has none by default.
  2. To record sound, open Capture → Screen recording and turn on Computer sound and Microphone. Both are off by default.
  3. Pick the format there too: MP4 for video up to 30 minutes or GIF for short clips up to a minute. GIFs have no sound.
  4. Press the recording key, select an area and press Enter. Stop with the Stop button, Esc or the hotkey again.

More about the options is on the screen recording page.

Recordings can highlight clicks and show key shortcuts: Capture → Screen recording → Highlight clicks and Show key shortcuts.

Scrolling capture

To capture a whole page, chat or document, assign a key to Scrolling capture in Settings → Hotkeys and scroll with the mouse wheel — BLNK stitches the frames for you. How it works and its limits are on the scrolling screenshot page.

Presets

A preset is a set of capture settings: frame, markup style, cursor, delay, auto-blur, format and save folder. Hotkeys, language and uploads are not part of presets.

  • Standard: your settings.
  • For chats: a smaller JPG, Enter copies.
  • For docs: PNG, a 16:9 frame, thick red arrows.
  • For design: PNG at full size, no cursor, no auto-blur.

Pick a preset in Settings → Capture, from the BLNK icon menu in the tray, or with the Next preset hotkey. Changes to the settings are saved into the selected preset. Save as new creates your own.

The Palette row there switches to your own eight colours. Your palette is remembered and saved in the preset.

Get a link

Open Settings → Upload, choose your storage and press the test button. After that, Ctrl+U uploads a screenshot and copies the link. Which storage works is covered on the private links page.

Where files go

  • Screenshots and recordings are saved to Pictures\Screenshots. You can choose another folder under Settings → Saving.
  • History, settings and logs stay in the BLNK data folder and are never sent anywhere. See the privacy page.

How much space BLNK uses

AppAbout 14 MB
WebView2About 60 MB more after you first open the settings or history
HistoryAbout 0.25 MB per entry. Up to 500 entries by default, about 130 MB
Screenshots and videosIn your folder, as big as the files themselves

The history limit — 100, 500, 1000 or 5000 entries — is under Settings → Saving → History. Old entries leave history by themselves, and the files in your screenshots folder stay.

Questions and answers

Do I need to run BLNK as administrator?

No. BLNK works with normal user rights.

Why doesn’t the recording hotkey work?

It has no default key. Set one in the settings under Hotkeys.

How much space does history take?

About 0.25 MB per entry. By default it keeps up to 500 entries, about 130 MB. You can change the limit in the settings.
