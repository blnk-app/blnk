How it works

  1. Press a hotkey

    PrtSc on Windows. Select an area, the whole screen or a window; the magnifier shows coordinates and pixel colour.

  2. Annotate right there

    Arrows, boxes, text, numbers, highlighter and blur appear over the selection itself, without opening an editor.

  3. Copy, save or share

    One click copies the image, saves it to a folder or uploads it to your storage and puts a private link in the clipboard.

Features

A screenshot in a second

Area, screen or window with one hotkey. Exact frame sizes, arrow-key nudging and a magnifier with HEX colour.

Annotate in place

Eleven tools: arrow, line, box, ellipse, pencil, text, numbers, highlighter, blur, pixelate and fill. Undo and redo.

Tab auto-blur

BLNK finds browser tabs and bookmarks in the screenshot and blurs them, so nothing extra ends up in a chat. Windows for now.

A private link

Upload to your own S3, Cloudflare R2, HTTP address or FTP. The file name is 22 random characters and the file carries no metadata.

History at hand

All screenshots in one window: reopen with editable annotations, copy the link or delete the file from the server.

Light and native

Written in Rust, no Electron. Pin a screenshot on top, drag it from the notification, print it. English, Ukrainian and Russian.

Your screenshots stay yours

BLNK collects nothing and sends nothing on its own. It only goes online to check for updates and to upload a screenshot where you tell it to.

Frequently asked questions

Is BLNK free?

Yes. Everything BLNK does today is free. There is no account, no ads and no trial period.

Does BLNK work on Mac?

BLNK runs on Windows 10 and 11 (x64 and ARM). The macOS version for Apple Silicon is in testing and will be available soon.

Where are my screenshots stored?

On your computer, in the folder you choose. The history is stored locally too. Nothing is uploaded unless you press Upload.

How do I share a screenshot as a link?

Connect your own storage in the settings: S3, Cloudflare R2, an HTTP address or FTP. After an upload, the link is copied to the clipboard. The file name is 22 random characters, so the link can't be guessed.

Can I hide private information?

Yes. Blur, pixelate and fill are applied to the pixels of the saved file and can't be undone by the recipient. On Windows, BLNK also blurs browser tabs and bookmarks automatically.

How is BLNK different from Lightshot and ShareX?

Like Lightshot, BLNK lets you annotate right over the selection, but links lead to your own storage instead of a shared public service. Compared with ShareX, BLNK is simpler: few settings, annotation in place, and a macOS version is on the way.

What are the default hotkeys?

On Windows: PrtSc for an area, Shift+PrtSc for the whole screen, Ctrl+PrtSc for a window. On macOS: Option+Shift+1, 2 and 3. All of them can be changed in the settings.

How does BLNK update?

BLNK checks GitHub for new versions and installs them after you confirm. Every installer is signed and the signature is verified before installing. Automatic checks can be turned off.
