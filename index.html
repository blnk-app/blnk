How it works

  1. Press a hotkey

    PrtSc on Windows. Select an area, the whole screen or a window; the magnifier shows coordinates and pixel colour.

  2. Annotate right there

    Arrows, boxes, text, numbers, highlighter and blur appear over the selection itself, without opening an editor.

  3. Copy, save or share

    One click copies the image, saves it to a folder or uploads it to your storage and puts a private link in the clipboard.

Features

Screen recording

Record any area of the screen to MP4 with computer sound and microphone, or to GIF. 30 or 60 frames per second, pause, and Esc to stop.

Annotate in place

Arrow, line, box, text, numbers, highlighter and hiding tools right over the selection. Ellipse, pencil and cropping in the editor window. Undo and redo.

Hide private data

Blur, pixelate or fill anything by hand. BLNK can also find browser tabs and bookmarks and blur them for you.

A private link

Upload screenshots and recordings to your own S3, Cloudflare R2, Google Drive, HTTP address, FTP or SFTP. The file name is 22 random characters and the file carries no metadata.

All features

History where annotations stay editable for 7 days, pinned screenshots, colour picker, print, drag from the notification and more.

Light and native

Written in Rust, without Electron. See what BLNK is built with and why it stays small.

Small by design

BLNK is a native Rust app. At idle it uses about 4.5 MB of memory, and the selection appears in about 80 ms.

How BLNK stays light →

Your screenshots stay yours

BLNK collects nothing and sends nothing on its own. It only goes online to check for updates and to upload a screenshot where you tell it to.

Frequently asked questions

How much does BLNK cost?

BLNK is in public beta right now. A subscription and a lifetime licence will come later, and we’ll publish the prices in advance.

Does BLNK work on Mac?

BLNK runs on Windows 10 and 11 (x64; ARM builds are available up to 0.1.0-beta.4). The macOS version for Apple Silicon is in testing and will be available soon.

Where are my screenshots stored?

On your computer, in the folder you choose. The history is stored locally too. Nothing is uploaded unless you send it yourself.

How do I share a screenshot as a link?

Connect your own storage in the settings: S3, Cloudflare R2, Google Drive, an HTTP address, FTP or SFTP. After an upload, the link is copied to the clipboard. The file name is 22 random characters, so the link can't be guessed.

Can I hide private information?

Yes. Blur, pixelate and fill change the pixels of the saved file. For passwords and card numbers use fill: it leaves nothing to recover. On Windows, BLNK can also blur browser tabs and bookmarks automatically.

How is BLNK different from other screenshot tools?

You annotate right over the selection, with no separate window. Links lead to your own storage instead of a shared public service. There are few settings, everything is at hand, and a macOS version is on the way.

What are the default hotkeys?

On Windows: PrtSc for an area, Shift+PrtSc for the whole screen, Ctrl+PrtSc for a window. On macOS: Option+Shift+1, 2 and 3. All of them can be changed in the settings; see the Hotkeys guide.

How does BLNK update?

BLNK checks GitHub for new versions and installs them after you confirm. Every update is signed and the signature is verified before installing. Automatic checks can be turned off.
