Free screen recorder for Windows

BLNK records any area of your screen to MP4 with computer sound and microphone, or to a short GIF. There is no account, watermark or trial period.

Recording options

FormatMP4 (H.264 video) or GIF
Frame rate30 or 60 frames per second for MP4, up to 15 for GIF
LengthUp to 30 minutes for MP4, up to a minute for GIF
SoundComputer sound and microphone, each turned on separately. GIFs have no sound
ControlsTimer, sound and microphone buttons, pause and stop. Esc also stops the recording
CountdownOptional 3-2-1 before recording, not included in the video
CursorShow or hide it in the recording
After recordingThe file is saved and added to history. You can upload it to your own storage right away and get a link

How to record your screen

  1. Open the BLNK settings and assign a hotkey to Record screen. It has no default hotkey.
  2. Press the hotkey and select the area you want to record.
  3. Press Enter or the record button.
  4. Use the panel to mute computer sound or the microphone, pause or stop. Esc or pressing the hotkey again also stops the recording.

Use 60 frames per second for scrolling and animation. The motion is smoother, but the file is larger. GIF suits short clips for chats: it is at most 960 pixels wide and opens anywhere as an image.

Sound in recordings

  • Computer sound is everything you hear from the speakers, notifications included.
  • Microphone is the default microphone in Windows.

Both are off by default. Turn them on in the settings under Capture → Screen recording. During a recording, each one can be muted with its button on the panel.

If something doesn’t work, see troubleshooting.

Questions and answers

Does BLNK record sound?

Yes, in MP4. Computer sound and the microphone are off by default. Turn them on in the settings under Capture → Screen recording. GIFs have no sound.

How long can a recording be?

Up to 30 minutes for MP4 and up to a minute for GIF. BLNK then stops by itself and keeps the file.

Will the recording frame appear in the video?

No. The frame, timer, countdown and control panel are not captured.

Does screen recording work on macOS?

Screen recording is currently available on Windows only.
