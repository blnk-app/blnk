Annotate screenshots right on the screen

BLNK shows its tools over the selection, so you can explain a screenshot before you copy, save or share it.

Eleven annotation tools

Most tools are on the panel next to the selection. The ellipse and pencil are in the editor window: choose Open in editor or press E.

ToolUse it toWhere
ArrowPoint to a button or detailIn the selection
LineConnect or underline elementsIn the selection
BoxFrame an areaIn the selection
EllipseCircle an elementIn the editor
PencilDraw freehandIn the editor
TextAdd a commentIn the selection
NumbersShow steps in orderIn the selection
HighlighterMark important textIn the selection
BlurSoften private detailsIn the selection
PixelateHide details with large pixelsIn the selection
FillCover data completelyIn the selection

How to annotate a screenshot

  1. Press PrtSc and select an area.
  2. Choose a tool on the panel next to the selection.
  3. Draw, then copy, save or upload the result.

To hide passwords and other sensitive data, read how to choose between blur, pixelate and fill.

Questions and answers

Do I need to open a separate editor?

No. You can annotate directly over the selection. When you need more, the editor window adds the ellipse, pencil and cropping.

Can I change annotations later?

Yes, for 7 days. Open the screenshot from the history and its annotations are still editable. This needs the Keep originals setting in the Saving section, which is on by default.

Can I undo a change?

Yes. Undo and redo are available while you annotate.
