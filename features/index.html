BLNK features

BLNK takes screenshots, records the screen, annotates, hides private data and creates private links, all from one small Windows app.

Main features

  • Screen recording: record any area to MP4 with sound, or to GIF.
  • Annotation: eleven tools, most of them right over the selection.
  • Hiding private data: blur, pixelate, fill and automatic blur for browser tabs.
  • Private links: upload to storage you control and get a link in the clipboard.
  • Speed and technology: what BLNK is built with and why it stays small.
