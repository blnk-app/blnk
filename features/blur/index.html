How to hide private data on a screenshot

Use fill for passwords and card numbers. Use blur or pixelate for less sensitive details, and let BLNK hide browser tabs automatically.

Blur, pixelate or fill

MethodBest forProtection
FillPasswords, card numbers, keysHighest
PixelateNames, faces, small detailsMedium
BlurBackground and minor detailsBasic

Automatic blur for browser tabs

BLNK can find browser elements in a screenshot and blur them for you. In the capture settings, choose what to hide: tabs, bookmarks, the address bar or your profile.

Automatic blur creates regular layers that you can move or delete. The feature works on Windows.

Originals on your computer

To let you re-edit a screenshot from the history, BLNK keeps its original without blur on your computer for 7 days. If you don't want that, turn off Keep originals in the Saving section of the settings.

Questions and answers

Which method is the safest?

Fill. It replaces the pixels with a solid colour, so nothing from the original remains in that area.

Can blurred text be recovered?

Blur and pixelate change the pixels, but large text can sometimes be partly reconstructed. Use fill for anything critical.

Which browsers does auto-blur support?

Chrome, Edge, Opera and Firefox on Windows.

Can I turn auto-blur off for one screenshot?

Yes. Press Ctrl+B in the selection to remove or restore all automatic blur layers.
