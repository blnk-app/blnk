BLNK changelog

New features and improvements in every public version of BLNK, newest first.

0.1.0-beta.12

22 November 2026

  • New: click highlighting and key shortcuts in screen recordings, for tutorials.
  • New: repeat the last area with Ctrl+Shift+PrtSc.
  • New: trim margins. Shift+C snaps the frame to the content, patterned backgrounds included.
  • New: a pixel ruler (M) and your own eight-colour palette.
  • Ctrl+D duplicates the selected shape in the selection window too.
  • The border around the recording and scrolling capture area is visible again.
  • Updates find the newest version however releases are sorted.

0.1.0-beta.11

22 November 2026

  • New: scrolling capture. Scroll a page or a chat, and BLNK stitches the frames into one screenshot.
  • New: presets, sets of capture settings you switch in the tray or with a hotkey.
  • Hotkeys with Ctrl, Alt, Shift or Win work over windows that run as administrator.
  • A double click opens a screenshot from history.

0.1.0-beta.10

20 November 2026

  • New: computer sound and microphone in screen recordings. Each one is turned on in the settings and muted with a button on the panel.
  • New: GIF recordings up to a minute, and Esc stops a recording.
  • New: uploads to Google Drive.
  • Screen recordings are now in history, where you can upload them and get a link.
  • A hotkey can be a single key. If Windows already uses a shortcut, BLNK suggests a free one with Ctrl+Alt.
  • A smooth countdown before recording.
  • White text in recordings no longer turns yellow.

0.1.0-beta.8

16 November 2026

  • New: screen recording to MP4.

0.1.0-beta.7

15 November 2026

  • Black-and-white logo, white selection frame and a more visible crosshair.
  • The update notice now waits for fonts before drawing its text.

0.1.0-beta.6

15 November 2026

  • The settings window opens without a white flash and is taller.
  • BLNK shows a notice after an update.

0.1.0-beta.5

15 November 2026

  • Toolbar size setting: small, medium, large or extra large.
  • Smaller panels and faster drawing of shapes.
  • The history window shows the screenshot count, limit and disk usage.
  • More reliable automatic blur on the first capture.

0.1.0-beta.4

14 November 2026

  • New: upload over SFTP with a password or key.
  • Choose hotkeys on the first-run screen.
  • Smaller PNG files and optional scaling down to 100%.
  • Clearer upload error messages.

0.1.0-beta.3

11 November 2026

  • Compact toolbars in the selection.
  • Selection frame colour setting.

0.1.0-beta.2

9 November 2026

  • New: automatic updates with signature verification.

0.1.0-beta.1

8 November 2026

  • First public version: screenshots, annotation, automatic blur, history and uploads to S3, HTTP and FTP.
